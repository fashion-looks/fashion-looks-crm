How to interpret the variance

Net issued = Fabric issued − Usable fabric returned
Marker-plan usage = Marker consumption per piece × Cut quantity
Actual consumption per piece = Net issued ÷ Cut quantity
Variance (MTR) = Net issued − Marker-plan usage
Variance % = Variance ÷ Marker-plan usage × 100
Cut completion % = Cut quantity ÷ Order quantity × 100

Data and control notes

This version is meter-based. Do not mix KG, yards or different measurement conventions in the same register. Use one record per PO/style/fabric lot/cutting batch. If a style has multiple marker versions, create separate records and retain the approved marker reference in your source documentation.

Only aggregate records with comparable fabric, marker and measurement conventions. The dashboard totals are useful for internal review, not a substitute for stock ledger, ERP or physical verification.
