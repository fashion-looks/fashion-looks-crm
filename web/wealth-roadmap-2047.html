Revenue ≠ profit ≠ net worth

Keep these three measures separate in your dashboard and accounts.

Business revenue

Total sales or service fees earned before expenses. Track billed revenue and cash collected separately.

Business profit

Revenue minus business costs, including relevant operating costs, wages, taxes and other expenses. Profit is not always cash in the bank.

Personal net worth

Personal assets plus defensible business-equity value, minus liabilities. Avoid double-counting business cash or investments.
